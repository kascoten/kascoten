Annual Events

At KASCOTE & KASCOTEN, we host a range of annual events designed to foster knowledge sharing, innovation, and collaboration within our community. These events bring together professionals, stakeholders, and partners to exchange ideas, celebrate achievements, and strengthen networks.

07 OCT 2017

2017 Annual Event

KASCOTEN’s 2017 Annual Event, held at Ni’ima Guest Palace in Nasarawa Kano, focused on attracting governments, cooperative organizations, and the general public towards patronizing locally made products for entrepreneurship development.
The event featured distinguished guest speakers, including Hon. Comm. of Finance Prof. Kabiru Dandago, MD ISPP Dr. Jibilla Muhammad, and special guest of honour His Excellency Dr. Abdullahi Umar Ganduje, alongside other notable dignitaries.

2024 Annual Event

KASCOTEN’s 2024 Annual Event was held at Khalifa Isyaku Rabiu University, Kano, bringing together traders, entrepreneurs, and stakeholders to promote innovation, collaboration, and the growth of local businesses.
The event featured insightful sessions, networking opportunities, and highlighted the importance of education and partnership in driving Kano’s economic development.
